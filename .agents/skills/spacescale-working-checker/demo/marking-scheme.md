# Marking scheme: sketching a quadratic

For \(y = x^2 + 7x + 10\):

1. Factorise: \((x + 2)(x + 5)\). One mark.
2. Roots at \(x = -2\) and \(x = -5\). One mark.
3. Check one root by substitution. One mark.
4. y-intercept at \(10\). One mark.
5. Sketch with the roots and intercept marked. One mark.

A student who marks the wrong roots but checks by substitution and notices
the contradiction should be led back to step 1, not told the roots.
